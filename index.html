About Amma Bojanam

Amma Bojanam, meaning "Mother's Food" in Telugu, is more than just a restaurant - it's a celebration of authentic Indian cuisine and the love that goes into every home-cooked meal.

Founded with the vision of bringing the warmth and flavors of traditional Indian cooking to our community, we take pride in serving dishes that remind you of your mother's kitchen.

Our chefs bring decades of experience and passion to create dishes that are not just delicious but also healthy and made with the finest ingredients.
